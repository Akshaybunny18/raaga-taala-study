Can Indian Classical Music
Sharpen Your Focus?

A cross-genre study examining how Raagas & Taalas compare to film music, Western music, and silence on human concentration and memory.

Background

What We're Studying

Raaga (melodic framework) and Taala (rhythmic cycle) are the twin pillars of Indian classical music, widely believed to influence mood and mental state — but direct empirical research is sparse. We're changing that.

🧠

Research Question 1

Do different Raagas and Taalas in Indian classical music measurably affect attention and memory?

🎬

Research Question 2

How does this effect compare to Indian film music and Western music under the same conditions?

🔍

Research Question 3

Does self-reported belief about music's effect on focus actually match measured cognitive performance?

How It Works

Study Methodology

01

Literature Review

Summarizing neurological and psychological research on Raagas, Taalas, and music's effect on focus across genres.

02

Intake Form

Participants register with their roll number, share their listening habits, and name 3 songs they like to work with.

03

Phase 1 — White Noise vs. Your Song

Two rounds, each with a randomly assigned test done twice: once with white noise, then (after a 3-minute break) a fresh version with a song of the participant's choosing. Mood checks and short reflections are recorded throughout.

04

Phase 2 — Music Conditions (coming soon)

A 4-round session comparing Indian classical, film, and Western music conditions.

Music Conditions

Phase 1: 🌫️ white noise vs. 🎵 the participant's own preferred song. Phase 2 (planned):

🎻 Indian Classical (Instrumental) 🎤 Indian Classical (Lyrical) 🎬 Indian Film Music 🎸 Western Music 🔇 Silence / White Noise

Cognitive Tasks (2 assigned at random per participant)

📖 Reading + Summary
Read a short essay (200–250 words), then write a summary from memory
⌨️ Reverse Typing
Type a paragraph with word order completely reversed
🟣 Dot Memory Puzzle
Memorize and reproduce 7 dot positions in a grid
🔄 Symmetry Test
Judge left-right symmetry of shapes against the clock
🔃 Rotated Image Test
Mentally rotate 3D-like shapes (rotated vs mirrored)
🔢 Number Sequence
10 random circles with 0–9 appear briefly, then click in order
🔤 Alphabet Speed Typing
Type A–Z as fast as possible; timer tracks your reaction & speed
