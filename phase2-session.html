📋 How Phase 2 Works

About 25–30 minutes in one sitting, including a 1-minute break between rounds. Please don't switch tabs during the tests.

  1. A short mood check (under a minute).
  3. 4 rounds of the same test, each with different audio — white noise, Western classical, Indian classical vocal and Indian classical instrumental, in a set order:
    • the round's audio plays for 30 seconds while you just listen,
    • then the test starts automatically, with the audio still playing,
    • followed by a short form about how you feel and how the round went.
  4. A few final questions about your experience.

Your progress is saved after every step — if something goes wrong, log in again with your roll number to continue.
