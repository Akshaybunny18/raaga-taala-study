Phase 2

Four Kinds of Music

Phase 2 is for everyone who finished Phase 1. You'll do one test four times, each time with different audio, and tell us how each round felt. It takes about 25–30 minutes in one sitting.

The four rounds

The same pieces are used for every participant. The order of the rounds differs from person to person.

🌫️ White noise 🎻 Western classical 🎤 Indian classical (vocal) 🪕 Indian classical (instrumental)

How it works

  1. Log in with the roll number you used in Phase 1, and check your headphones.
  2. A short mood check, then a few practice tries of your test (no audio, not scored).
  3. Four rounds. Each one plays its audio for 30 seconds while you just listen, then the test starts with the audio still playing, followed by a short form about how you feel. There is a 1-minute break between rounds.
  4. A few final questions comparing the four rounds.

You'll need headphones, a quiet place, and the same device you'd normally study on. Your progress is saved after every step.
