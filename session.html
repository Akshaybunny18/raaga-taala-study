📋 How Phase 1 Works

About 20–25 minutes in one sitting. Please don't switch tabs during the tests.

  1. A short mood check (under a minute).
  2. Round 1 — a test, done twice:
    • first with white noise, followed by 2 quick questions,
    • a 3-minute break where you preview and pick one of your 3 songs,
    • then a different version of the same test with your song, followed by a short form.
  3. Round 2 — the same process with a different test.
  4. A few final questions about your experience.

Audio starts when you press Start and stops when the test ends. Your progress is saved after every step — if something goes wrong, log in again with your roll number to continue.
